# Dogear — User Guide

A fast, distraction-free ebook reader. *Pick up right where you left off.*

---

## Adding books

Tap **Add (＋)** in the library:
- **Import files** — pick one or many books (EPUB, PDF, TXT, CBZ).
- **Import folder** — import every supported book in a folder (and its subfolders).
- **Web upload** — send books from a computer or phone over your Wi‑Fi (see below).

You can also import a **ZIP** of books — Dogear extracts the supported ones automatically.
Duplicate files (same contents) are skipped.

Empty library? Tap **Add sample books** to explore the shelf right away.

## The shelf

- Switch between **grid** and **list** with the view button.
- **Sort** by recently read, recently added, title, author, file size, or progress.
- **Filter** by reading status (Unread / Reading / Finished / Abandoned).
- **Search** title, author, and tags from the search button.
- Tap a book for details; long actions (edit, replace cover, refresh, delete) live in its
  **⋮** menu.

## Reading

Open a book and tap **Read**. The page is distraction-free — no permanent controls.

- **Tap the left/right edge** to turn pages. **Tap the center** for the menu.
- **Bottom slider**: drag to jump anywhere in the book.
- **Top menu**: bookmark, search-in-book (reflowable formats), reading settings (Aa), and a
  ⋮ menu for Contents, Bookmarks, Highlights, and Notes.

A short tutorial appears the first time you open a book (you can turn it off in Settings).

### Hardware keyboard
←/Page Up = previous · →/Page Down/Space = next · Home/End = start/end of book.
Turn on **Volume keys turn pages** in the reading settings if you prefer.

### Reading settings (Aa)
Theme (Cream, Sepia, Dark, AMOLED, and more, plus a custom theme), font and size, weight,
line and paragraph spacing, margins, alignment, hyphenation, page animation, brightness
(reader/system/auto), keep-screen-awake, and orientation lock. Changes apply instantly.

### Bookmarks, highlights, notes
- **Bookmark** the current spot with the bookmark button; manage them from the ⋮ menu.
- **Highlight**: select text, then ⋮ → *Highlight selection* (reflowable formats).
- **Notes**: add a note at your position; export all notes/highlights via **Share**.

Your position is saved continuously and survives crashes — you always reopen exactly where
you stopped.

## Web upload

Settings → or Add (＋) → **Web upload**:
1. Optionally set a port and password, then **Start server**.
2. On another device on the **same Wi‑Fi**, open the shown address (or scan the QR code).
3. Choose files and upload — they appear in your library automatically.

The server is reachable only on your local network and runs until you stop it.

## Settings

Theme mode (light/dark/system), color palette, app font, default reader font, the tutorial
toggle, **cover-cache** size and clear, and **Backup & Restore**.

### Backup & restore
**Export backup** writes your library metadata, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights,
notes, collections, tags, and settings to a JSON file. **Restore** reads it back. (The book
files themselves aren't inside the backup — on the same device their stored locations still
work; to move everything to a new device, copy your books across too.)

## Privacy

Dogear is offline-first and account-free. Nothing leaves your device except what you
explicitly send over your own Wi‑Fi with the upload server. Books never run their own scripts
or load anything from the internet while you read.
